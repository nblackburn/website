As I sit at my desk reflecting on life, work and the wider world, I feel a heavy
sense of unease at the way the world is changing:

- Solving problems - gone
- Quality assurance - who cares?
- Working together - eliminated
- Learning skills - why bother?

As I fight to hold on to some of what I hold near and dear, I see everyone else
around me losing the battle and all for the sake of progress.

---

## **Nogress**

> “Movement without a goal or purpose”

There is something very wrong with this new idea of progress as it has movement
without a destination. Instead, it has us racing under the cover of night
without a licence trying to hit partially constructed checkpoints.

Without a fixed measure, there is no sense of momentum nor is there a means of
knowing how far you have come.

## **Progress for whom?**

It doesn’t matter if you trained for 10 years because now it can be done in ten
minutes. It doesn’t matter if you haven’t trained at all because your checkpoint
is always one step ahead from where you are. Nobody wins when there is no time
to discover, reflect and celebrate what you and others have achieved because
none of that matters any more.

> Progress is no longer for you, but for those who seek control.

## **The real progress**

The very reason we can go from “10 years” to “10 minutes” is because of the
people put in the time to make that possible. To put it plainly, progress needs
knowledge which is formed from discovery.

You matter, your craft matters so hold on to it and NEVER give up hope. The very
fact we keep creating, discovering and learning in this climate is itself
progress and will always have value because without progress, nogress can never
happen.

## **Why we need progress**

We require progress now more than ever because the gears of our society keep
turning. With each new generation, new problems surface, often ones it had no
hand in creating. In order to solve those problems we need to look at the
mistakes that were made and learn from them.

Mistakes help us toward progress. As such, they play a critical role in our
world which is why it’s important we are able to make them because without them,
we don’t learn and when we don’t learn we don’t progress.
